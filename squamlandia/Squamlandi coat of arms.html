The coat of arms

The Squamlani coat of arms is the official device of Squamlandia . It consists of a shield of squamish oats with the national animal of Squamlandia, the goat, in the center. The shield rises from the waves, flanked by two leaping squamish bass. Above the shield is a knight's helmet representing the self-determination of the squamish people, wreathed with the distinctive blue seaweed that is found only in the squamish sea. Its crest is a sprig of squamish cumin.

Below is a scroll with the latin motto of Squamlandia, "In Sqam Terra,omnis island narratio", which means "In squamlandia, every island tells its own story."

The official name is the Squamlandi coat of arms, this being the adjective used for formal reference to the Squamish nation and its people.
